ATO + AIHW / public area data

Turn a postcode cohort into places worth investigating.

A practical guide to the taxation and Medicare datasets already used in postcode modelling—where they live, what they contain, and how to use them without overstating what they mean.

Documentation only · no government or user data included

Two sources. Two different populations.

ATO is genuinely postcode-based but describes tax lodgers. AIHW describes Medicare-subsidised care at SA3 or PHN level. Both can enrich a postcode cohort, but only if their native geography and population are preserved.

From cohort to similar postcodes

Similarity is a repeatable screening method, not a prediction by itself. Keep the transformation explicit so another analyst can reproduce or challenge it.

01AggregateCount cohort members by postcode; remove invalid values and enforce a privacy threshold.
02EnrichJoin ATO by postcode. Map POA to SA3 before joining AIHW.
03NormaliseChoose one period/cohort; derive rates and medians; robust-scale features.
04MatchBuild the cohort profile and calculate weighted distance to other postcodes.
05ValidateTest sensitivity, inspect counts/flags, then conduct local market research.
A

Align definitions

Never mix taxable-only and all-individual rows, or different years, without explicitly harmonising them.

B

Prefer rates over totals

Totals mostly rediscover postcode size. Keep counts as reliability fields rather than similarity drivers.

C

Preserve missingness

Privacy suppression, non-publication and invalid geography are not observations of zero.

D

Standardise features

Without scaling, dollar measures dominate percentage and service-rate measures.

E

Test sensitivity

Re-run with different feature groups and weights. Strong candidates should remain reasonably stable.

F

Avoid individual inference

Area statistics cannot reveal a cohort member’s income, tax position, health status or service use.

The geography bridge

“Postcode” is not one universal boundary system. Store each source’s native key and make the crosswalk a visible, versioned part of the analysis.

Input / ATO

Postcode or ABS POA

ATO values can be joined using postcode text, while acknowledging that return-address postcodes are not guaranteed to equal ABS POAs.

→
AIHW publication

ASGS 2021 SA3

Use an official correspondence or map a POA representative point to an SA3 polygon. Retain the method and boundary flags.

A centroid crosswalk is practical but can disagree with AIHW’s weighted allocation for postcodes crossing multiple areas. Treat the attached value as SA3-level ecological context.

Proven baseline: the Cash Savvy pawn-location research parsed the ATO 2023–24 postcode workbook, mapped ABS 2021 POA centroids into SA3 boundaries, and attached AIHW 2023–24 GP indicators. The added sources were useful descriptive context but did not improve grouped out-of-sample model performance—evidence that more data does not automatically mean a better model.
